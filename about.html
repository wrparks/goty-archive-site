About the archive

A collective picture of the year in games.

GOTY Archive combines year-end video game lists from professional publications, individual critics, communities, and players. Each list contributes points according to a consistent scoring system. The goal is to preserve the lists themselves while presenting a combined view.

Eligibility & list formats

All games are eligible regardless of release year. The year shown is the year the list was published, not necessarily the game’s release year. Ranked and unranked editorial lists are accepted. Award results are archived separately: only an explicitly named overall Game of the Year winner earns 10 points; category winners are preserved without aggregate points.

Scoring methodology

Ranked lists: 1st = 10 points · 2nd = 8 · 3rd = 6 · 4th–10th = 5 · 11th–25th = 3 · 26th and lower = 1.
Unranked lists: 10 games or fewer = 5 points per game · 11–25 = 3 · more than 25 = 1.
Award results: an explicitly named overall Game of the Year winner = 10 points; category winners and nominees = 0 points and are retained for reference.

Tie-breaking

Equal point totals are ordered by first-place finishes, then total list appearances, then alphabetical game title.
