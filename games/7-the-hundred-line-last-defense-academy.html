About the Game

The Hundred Line: Last Defense Academy is a RPG-strategy game developed by Media.Vision and Too Kyo Games. It came out on 24-04-2025. It was published by Aniplex. The game is rated as "Recommended" on RAWG. You can play The Hundred Line: Last Defense Academy on PC and Nintendo Switch. You can purchase the game on Nintendo eShop. It was produced by Shuntaro Inou. It was directed by Kazutaka Kodaka and Kotaro Uchikoshi. It was scored by Jun Fukuda and Masafumi Takada.
